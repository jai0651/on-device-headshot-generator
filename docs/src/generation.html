The loop, call by call

All generation goes through one hand-written function, manual_loop.generate(). Stage 1 proved it produces pixel-identical output to the diffusers library, and stage 5 proved the same again with FaceID and ControlNet switched on. So this is exactly what runs, not a simplification.

Why CFG doubles the bill

Every step runs the UNet on the prompt and on the negative prompt, then extrapolates: noise = uncond + 6·(cond − uncond). It's what makes the image obey the prompt, and it's why 25 steps is 50 UNet calls. Distilled models (stage 8, LCM) bake guidance in and drop it to 1×.

Why latents

The loop never touches pixels. A 512×512×3 image is 786,432 numbers; its latent is 16,384, 48× fewer. Only the VAE decoder at the very end works at full resolution, which is why it's cheap in time but heavy in memory.

Four ways to steer the same loop

Identity and layout aren't separate pipelines. They're optional extra inputs to the same UNet call (3c above), which is why they can be combined.

The pose template

Rather than copy a pose from some photo, the skeleton is placed where the compliance checks want a head: eye line 62% up from the bottom, head about 60% of the frame height, centred, shoulders level. It's drawn in the exact OpenPose colour format the ControlNet was trained on.

The control image: 18 COCO keypoints, only the head and shoulders present.

What it produces (8-step validation run): whole head in frame, centred, square to camera.

The knobs, and what they did

Stage 1 swept guidance strength and samplers on one seed. The CFG sweep is what set 6.0 as the project's default: below ~4 the prompt is ignored, above ~10 skin turns to wax.

Guidance 1 → 15, two seeds. Same noise in every column; only the guidance changes.

Six samplers × 10 / 20 / 30 steps. DPM++ 2M Karras is clean by 20, so 25 is the project default.

Next: the edit path (planned, stage 5c)

Everything above generates a face. The edit path keeps the real one: segment face and hair from the selfie, inpaint everything else with SD 1.5's inpainting UNet plus pose control, then a low-strength img2img pass (0.2–0.35) over the whole image to match the lighting. Identity is then preserved by construction, and low strength means only a fraction of the 25 steps run.
